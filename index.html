MapMind — Free Online Mind Mapping & Diagram Canvas

The fast, private, and AI-powered diagram workspace for thinkers, creators, and engineers.

Key Features

  • AI Chat to Mind Map: Paste structured responses from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or DeepSeek and instantly convert them to interactive visual graphs.
  • Intelligent Auto-Layout: Powered by Dagre and ELK algorithms for hierarchical, radial, and balanced node distribution.
  • Keyboard-First Speed: Create child nodes (Tab), sibling nodes (Enter), and navigate your maps without taking your hands off the keyboard.
  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: Your data remains exclusively in your browser's IndexedDB storage with zero tracking or telemetry.
  • Vector Export: Export your mind maps as high-resolution PNG, SVG, or PDF formats.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a mind map online for free?

Open MapMind in any modern browser, double-click or use Tab/Enter to create nodes, or use the AI import tool to convert notes into diagrams instantly.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No. MapMind requires no account, sign-up, or installation. It works directly in your web browser.
